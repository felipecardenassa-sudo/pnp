## How Felipe wants the work done
- **Simple and fact-only.** No fluff and no assumptions. Fact-check every number against its source before the final version, and say plainly what you don't know.
- **Explanations stay inside the deliverable.** Lines such as "where the numbers come from", "how to check them yourself" or "nothing needs to be saved" count as fluff.
- **"Explain it like a toddler"** means a plain analogy plus the exact clicks.
- **"Tell me before adding it to the slide"** means you answer in chat and change nothing until he agrees.
- **Spreadsheets** must work in Google Sheets (the team uses only Google Workspace):
  - plain SUM, IF and SUMIFS, with no XLOOKUP and no macros;
  - freeze header rows only, and put charts under the tables;
  - plain column or line charts;
  - very easy to update, visual and clear;
  - detailed, but never messy.
- **PDFs** are short and visual. Each one covers what's flagged, what's working, what can be improved and the next steps.
- **Presentations for Raúl and Tereza.** Both have run paid media and dislike fluff, Raúl most of all.
  - Never explain basics such as what an ad or an ad set is.
  - Make them very visual:
    - each channel's structure as a diagram with counts (campaigns → ad sets → ads → landing pages);
    - what's going well and what's going wrong;
    - proposed structures as mock-up blocks, with no ad copy;
    - before → after numbers;
    - landing pages as annotated page images;
    - a 7/30/60/90-day test plan.
  - Never use on slides:
    - charts, unless he asks;
    - source notes or footnotes ("this came from…");
    - any mention of the research playbook;
    - HubSpot data, in a paid media deck.
  - Use cost per lead (CPL). ROAS can't be measured until deal values are tied to campaigns.
- **Scope.** Paid strategy covers Meta, Google Search and ChatGPT Ads.
  - YouTube only exists because the CEO wants cheap views. Leave it out of audits, decks and plans, but keep it in the weekly report.
  - LinkedIn is off for now (Raúl: too expensive).
  - The audit deck covers Meta and Google Search only.
