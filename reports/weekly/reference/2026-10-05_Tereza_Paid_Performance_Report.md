**1. Executive Summary**

Combined paid spend: \$1,174.12 (Meta, USD) and €638.05 (Google Search
€501.48 + YouTube €136.57, EUR). WoW: Meta −51.2%, Google/YouTube −8.5%
(€697.56 → €638.05, incl. last week's new YouTube campaign). Meta halved
because the FR_Alibaba traffic campaign wound down; the lead engine
itself was flat on spend and delivered more.

**What improved**

-   **Meta leads 89 → 97 (+9.0%);** cost per lead \$12.26 → \$11.06
    (−9.8%) on 1.7% less lead-engine spend (\$1,090.78 → \$1,072.46).
    The four US prospecting campaigns recovered: 29 → 38 leads, CPL
    \$17.75 → \$13.05.

-   **Google Search conversions 18 → 27 (+50%);** CPA €28.25 → €18.57
    (−34%) on 1.4% less spend (€508 → €501). Impressions +41% and CPC
    −30% (€2.42 → €1.69): delivery has come back.

-   **MQLs 41 → 52 (+27%);** Direct +75% (12 → 21) and Organic +54% (13
    → 20).

-   **Organic:** GSC clicks 7,883 (+2.3%) on impressions +17.7%; US
    clicks +9.5%; November Summit page clicks +38% (301 → 414).

**What declined**

-   **Paid MQLs fell:** Paid Social 9 → 3 and Paid Search/other flat at
    3; paid is 6 of 52 MQLs (12%) vs 12 of 41 (29%). Paid Social cost
    per MQL \~\$357 vs \~\$121 last week. The 1--2 week lag from
    high-volume lead weeks has not shown up yet.

-   **MQL quality weaker:** 17 of 52 MQLs disqualified (33%) vs 7 of 38
    (18%) in last week's report. Direct (8) and Organic (6) account for
    14 of the 17.

-   **GSC CTR 2.61% → 2.27%** as impressions grew faster than clicks
    (+17.7% vs +2.3%). Homepage clicks −6.5%; Japan −1.8% after last
    week's surge.

-   **YouTube:** 8th consecutive week with zero conversions. Spend down
    27% to €136.57 because the US-UK-FR-IT campaign (launched 28 Sep)
    recorded no spend this week.

**Changed significantly**

-   **Meta traffic buying has ended.** FR_Alibaba spend \$1,276 → \$67
    (141,717 impressions vs 2.46M). Account CPM \$0.96 → \$5.67,
    frequency 3.33 → 1.27 and link clicks 40,414 → 3,339 reflect that
    removal, not performance. The campaign still shows ACTIVE.

-   **Data flag:** last week's MQL count is restated from 38 to 41 in
    your table (CRM sync), so all MQL WoW figures here use restated
    numbers. No SQL file was supplied, so SQLs are not reported.
