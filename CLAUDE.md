## Rules for every deliverable
- Keep it simple and fact-only, with no fluff and no assumptions. Fact-check every number before the final version.
- Spreadsheets must work in Google Sheets (the team uses Google Workspace only). Use plain SUM, IF and SUMIFS, and no XLOOKUP or macros. Lessons from the tracker: never freeze more than the header rows (Sheets pushes charts below the frozen area, so charts go under the tables); conditional formatting can't reference another tab (use a helper cell on the same tab); keep charts to plain column/line types; keep formulas in the low thousands.
- PDFs should be short and visual. Each one covers what's flagged, what's working, what can be improved and next steps.
- Presentations for managers: Raúl and Tereza have both run paid media and dislike fluff (Raúl most of all), so never explain basics such as what an ad or ad set is (Felipe, 14 Oct 2026). Keep it short, to the point and easy to scan. Show each channel's structure as a diagram with counts (campaigns → ad sets → ads → landing pages), mark what's going well and what's going wrong on it, then the proposed structure with before → after numbers. Make it very visual (Felipe, 14 Oct 2026): per channel, the campaigns that work (structure, spend, who we targeted, why it works, how to improve it), what replaces the ones that don't, proposed structures as mock-up blocks (campaign → ad sets → creatives → landing page, no ad copy), landing pages as annotated page images, and a 7/30/60/90-day test plan. Frame Meta around Andromeda (the set-up is pre-Andromeda: many small ad sets on small budgets never leave learning). No charts unless asked, no playbook, source notes or footnotes on slides ("this came from…"), no HubSpot data in paid media decks; use CPL, since ROAS can't be measured until deal values are tied to campaigns.
- HubSpot access is **read-only audit**. Never create, edit, delete, send or change anything there.
- Follow GDPR:
  - Deliverables contain aggregates only, with counts of 1–4 shown as "<5".
  - Exception (Felipe, 14 Oct 2026): the weekly performance report shows exact HubSpot counts, as Tereza's report did. For that report the rule is "do as Tereza did".
  - Raw contact exports stay in the scratchpad and are deleted afterwards. Never commit them.
